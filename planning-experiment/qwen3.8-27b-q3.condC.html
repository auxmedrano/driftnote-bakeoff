What it does

Built to feel like paper — and work like a database.

Six things Driftnote does differently, so your notes stay yours and stay fast.

Local-first by default

Everything is written to disk on your machine first. The app is fully usable offline — the network is just a bonus, never a dependency.

End-to-end encrypted sync

When you opt in to sync, changes are encrypted before they leave your device. No one — including us — can read your notes in transit or at rest.

Instant full-text search

Search across every note, attachment, and tag in milliseconds. Results appear as you type, ranked the way you actually write.

Markdown, not a black box

Your notes are plain text you can open in any editor. Link, embed, and structure freely — the format is yours to keep forever.

Backups that make sense

Scheduled local snapshots with version history. Roll back a note to any moment, or export a clean archive whenever you like.

Keyboard-first

Everything is reachable without touching the mouse. Command palette, shortcuts, and full arrow-key navigation for power writers.
