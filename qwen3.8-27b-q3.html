Built local-first

Everything you need. None of the wait.

Driftnote is engineered around a single idea: your words should load instantly and never leave your hands without permission.

Instant by design

Opens in milliseconds and edits without a network. Every keystroke lands locally first — the cloud is an option, not a prerequisite.

Yours, encrypted

Notes live in a file on your device and can be sealed with end-to-end encryption. Export to Markdown or PDF whenever you like — no lock-in, ever.

Sync on your terms

Pick your own server, use a folder you control, or keep it entirely offline. Conflict resolution is built in, so edits never vanish silently.
